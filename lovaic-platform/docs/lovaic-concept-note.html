The one-liner

What is LOVAIC?

LOVAIC is a computer-vision platform, built on RLAI's proprietary engine, that reads live video from any source and converts it into structured insight and recommended actions — the way a single library like YOLO powers many tasks, but delivered as a finished, deployable product.

It ships in two editions off the same engine:

GOVERNMENT AI Intel City

A civic command centre for smart cities, municipal bodies and police.

ENTERPRISE Vision Suite

Operational intelligence for retail, hospitality, BFSI and manufacturing.

Our edge

Beyond a bounding box

Most "AI camera" pitches just draw boxes on a frame. LOVAIC's differentiators are what let us win the room — and they hold up in the field, not only in a slide.

Pixel-level, not just boxes

We segment objects at the pixel — capturing exact shape, overlap and partial occlusion. That means higher-fidelity understanding than box-only detectors.

Privacy by design

Faces and people can be redacted on the frame itself. Raw identities never need to leave the customer's premises — DPDP / GDPR friendly out of the box.

Sovereign & on-prem

Runs in the customer's own data centre or sovereign cloud, fully air-gap capable. Their footage and models stay under their control.

Works with existing cameras

No new hardware. We connect to the CCTV, NVR, drone or satellite feeds already in place — even low-resolution ones.

Proprietary RLAI engine

A multi-model orchestration stack with domain reasoning tuned per vertical — not a single off-the-shelf model bolted onto a dashboard.

One engine, many missions

Footfall, safety, traffic, waste, PPE, retail — the same platform, re-purposed per camera. New use-cases without new systems.

Objection handling

Sales talking points

"Do we need to buy new cameras?"

No. LOVAIC connects to the cameras and NVR the customer already has — even older, low-res feeds.

"Where does our footage go?"

Nowhere they don't want. It can run fully on-premise / sovereign, with on-frame privacy redaction — data stays under their control.

"Isn't this just another YOLO wrapper?"

No. We work at the pixel level, add domain reasoning per vertical, and deliver a deployable product — not a box-drawing demo.

"How quickly can we see value?"

We start with a 3–4 camera pilot connected remotely or on-site, prove the use-case in days, then scale across the estate.

Engagement model

Start small, prove value fast, then scale.

Land with a focused pilot on the customer's highest-value cameras — footfall at an entrance, safety at a gate. Once the value is visible on their own feeds, expand across the site and add new missions on the same engine. Deploy in the cloud, or fully on-premise for sovereign customers.
